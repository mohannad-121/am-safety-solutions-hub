## AMBS Strengths

1. Clean technical architecture with static output for indexable routes.
2. Strong UAE/Dubai topical coverage across safety nets, edge protection and temporary works.
3. Technical resources connect products, standards, RAMS, inspection and conversion paths.
4. Structured data matches visible page content and avoids fake ratings.
5. Quote and contact flows support project drawings and technical enquiries.
6. Admin/CMS capabilities support ongoing content and media maintenance.

## Remaining Weaknesses

1. Real AMBS project photography and permission-approved case studies remain the largest trust gap.
2. Named technical authors, engineers and verified credentials are absent.
3. Placeholder RAMS downloads need approved branded PDFs.
4. Live Google Business Profile and citation consistency have not been audited.
5. Production Core Web Vitals and Google Search Console data are not available in the repository.

## Why TSS and Other Competitors Rank

1. Long operating history and established brand recognition.
2. Real product photography and installation visuals.
3. Product catalogues and technical downloads.
4. Detailed safety-net fan, edge-protection and loading-platform variants.
5. EN 1263 and EN 13374 terminology.
6. UAE/Dubai contact and service-area signals.
7. Client logos and visible project proof.
8. Technical specifications such as capacities, dimensions and configurations.
9. Product-level internal linking.
10. Direct consultation and enquiry calls to action.

## Next Evidence Needed From AMBS

- Approved project names, locations and client permissions.
- Original installation and inspection photographs.
- Product certificates, test reports and current catalogue revisions.
- Team names, roles and verified qualifications.
- Approved testimonials and client logos.
- Final branded RAMS checklist PDFs.
- Google Business Profile access or exported listing data.

No unverified project, certification, review, client or No. 1 claim should be published.
